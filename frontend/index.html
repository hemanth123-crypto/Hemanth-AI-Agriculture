      Reviewer FAQ: How is Risk Analysis Formulated & Computed?
      
            
      
            Academic Viva Guide
          
      

          
      
            
      1
      
            
      
              Multi-Agent Guardrail Architecture:
              
      
                The RiskAnalysisAgent performs non-linear boundary checking against empirical crop physiological tolerance curves (pH thresholds, drought/flood limits, heat/frost extremes) and guarantees safety sanitization.
              
      
            
      
          
      

          
      
            
      2
      
            
      
              Liebig's Law of the Minimum for Nutrients:
              
      
                Crop yield and health are bounded by the scarcest nutrient resource (N, P, or K). The deficit index penalizes sub-optimal soil concentrations proportionally before yield regression.
              
      
            
      
          
      

          
      
            
      3
      
            
      
              Grounding with Historical Evidence:
              
      
                Risk scores are corroborated with the 1,500 historical empirical records in FAISS. If similar past cases experienced crop failure under the same conditions, the risk classification is elevated to prevent hallucination.
